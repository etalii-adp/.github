## A Different Perspective - ADP

The time is ready for a new perspective on information and knowledge, and the ADP initiative aims to provide 'A Different Perspective' on matters by introducing a range of specialized diagram and text designers. The aim is to not be generic and universal but rather highly tuned and specialized for specific tasks, including (constructive technology) assessment, collaboration between human and agents and bringing clarity to textual data.
